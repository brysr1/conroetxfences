    <h2>How to choose a style for a Conroe-area lot</h2>
    <p>Begin with what the fence needs to do, then match it to the lot and the rules that apply.</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Small backyards in newer subdivisions.</strong> On 40- to 60-foot lots with houses close together, privacy or semi-privacy is the usual choice. These communities often have HOA guidelines, and we confirm the current ones before you settle on a design.</li>
      <li><strong>Lots backing to a lake, pond, or greenbelt.</strong> Rear fences on these lots are often open designs like ornamental metal, which keeps the view. Lake Conroe lakefront lots also need the SJRA line checked first.</li>
      <li><strong>Wooded acre lots.</strong> Long, uneven lines through trees work well with split rail, post-and-rail, or wire, with a privacy section close to the house.</li>
      <li><strong>Acreage with animals.</strong> Pasture and horse lines north toward Huntsville and east toward Cleveland need livestock-rated fencing and wide gates. See <a href="{{ '/materials/farm-ranch/' | relative_url }}">farm &amp; ranch</a>.</li>
      <li><strong>Pools and dogs.</strong> The style comes second to the job here: a pool barrier needs the right height, gaps, and gate hardware, and a dog fence has to stop climbing and digging.</li>
    </ul>
