faqs:
  - q: "What height and stain color does my HOA allow?"
    a: "That depends on your community, and sometimes on the section or lot. We don't guess. We look up the current guidelines for your lot, or ask the management company, before we quote, so the fence we price is one your committee can approve."
  - q: "Will you submit the application for me?"
    a: "We put together the site sketch, material and height details, and color information most committees ask for. Many communities want the homeowner to submit it through their portal, and we help you do that if needed. We don't start building until approval is in hand."
  - q: "My builder fence is only a few years old. Why is it leaning already?"
    a: "Builder fences are usually built to a tight budget, often with lighter posts set at modest depth. Wet clay, sprinkler overspray, and storm winds find those weak spots fast. We can repair it to match or replace it with heavier posts in the approved style."
  - q: "My lot backs to a pond or greenbelt. Is that different?"
    a: "Usually. Communities often limit rear fences on lots that face water, trails, or open space, and many want an open style such as ornamental metal there. We confirm what applies to your lot before we draw anything."
