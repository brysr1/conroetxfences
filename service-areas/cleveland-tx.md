      <h2>Cleveland and the land around it</h2>
      <p>Cleveland is a Liberty County city about 30 miles east of Conroe on SH 105. It incorporated in 1935, counted 7,471 people at the 2020 census, and was estimated at about 9,900 in 2025. The Sam Houston National Forest sits just to the north.</p>
      <p>Roads here converge from every direction. I-69/US 59 passes the city, with US 59 Business running through town as Washington Ave. SH 105 comes in from Conroe, SH 321 heads south toward Dayton, and FM 1010 and FM 787 carry traffic out to the subdivisions and farmland.</p>
      <p>What sets Cleveland apart for fencing is the land. Several of the subdivisions around Cleveland sell lots rather than finished homes, and owners place a manufactured home or build their own. Former rice fields and pine woods are being split into half-acre and larger lots, and the first things many owners need are a perimeter fence, a gate wide enough for a truck, and a fenced yard for kids and dogs.</p>

      <h2>City, county, and deed restrictions</h2>
      <p>Property inside Cleveland city limits answers to the city. Most of the rural subdivisions around it are in unincorporated Liberty County, and some extend into neighboring counties. Some subdivisions have an HOA or POA, some list none, and recorded deed restrictions can apply even where there is no association.</p>
      <p>We don't assume. For every address, we confirm with the City of Cleveland or the county what applies, check for deed restrictions, and request Texas 811 locates before we dig.</p>
