      <h2>Willis and the east shore</h2>
      <p>Willis is a small Montgomery County city on I-45, north of Conroe. The Census Bureau estimated about 7,650 residents in mid-2025, but the city's reach is bigger than its population. Thousands of homes along the east side of Lake Conroe carry Willis addresses and send their kids to Willis ISD, even where they sit outside the city itself.</p>
      <p>Two roads do most of the work. FM 1097 (W. Montgomery St. in town) runs west from I-45 exit 94 to the Lake Conroe bridge, past <a href="/neighborhoods/point-aquarius/">Point Aquarius</a> and <a href="/neighborhoods/lake-conroe-hills/">Lake Conroe Hills</a>. FM 830, off exit 92, leads west to <a href="/neighborhoods/seven-coves/">Seven Coves</a> and frames the edge of <a href="/neighborhoods/the-woodlands-hills/">The Woodlands Hills</a>. SH 75 parallels the interstate through town.</p>
      <p>Most of what we fence here falls into three groups. Lake subdivisions from the 1970s through the 2000s have quarter-acre lots, fenced back yards, and side gates wide enough for a boat trailer. Canal-front and bulkheaded lots raise the question of where the fence stops. North toward New Waverly, wooded one-acre lots call for perimeter lines and drive gates.</p>
