      <h2>No HOA, plenty of trees, and fences from another era</h2>
      <p>Cross the railroad tracks on Woodson Road east of I-45 and you are in Lake Chateau Woods. It was the original subdivision of what used to be the City of Chateau Woods. Homes went up here from the 1960s on, with most of the current houses dating from about 1976 to 1980. The area incorporated in 1975 and voted to dis-incorporate in 1993, when a MUD took over.</p>
      <p>The result is a neighborhood that feels different from the master-planned communities around it. There is no gate and, according to listings, no HOA fees. Lots vary a lot in size, from small lots around 0.15 acre to half-acre parcels, and many are shaded by pines and hardwoods.</p>
      <p>Wikipedia puts the Chateau Woods area about 10 miles south of Conroe, right next to The Woodlands. For us that means a short drive down I-45 from the office.</p>
