      <h2>Where the fence keeps horses in</h2>
      <p>Red Oak Ranch is small and spread out: about 49 homes, nearly all custom, on wooded lots that average 5 acres. It sits south-southwest of Conroe in the 77384 ZIP. From I-45 you take FM 1488 west, then Old Conroe Rd to a private gated entrance at Lake Forest Dr.</p>
      <p>The community has a horse arena and riding trails, and at least one property listed for sale has a four-stall barn. A fence here may need to contain a horse, divide a pasture from a yard, or keep a dog away from the barn. It also has to hold up in shade, under falling limbs, and on wet ground.</p>
      <p>Most homes date from the late 1990s, so fencing from that era may be ready for reset posts or rebuilt runs.</p>
