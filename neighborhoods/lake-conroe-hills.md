      <p>A lot of fence lines in Lake Conroe Hills do not end at a neighbor&rsquo;s fence. They end at a canal, a bulkhead, or a dock. The community sits on the Lake Conroe shoreline in Willis, reached from I-45 by heading west on FM 1097 and turning onto Cude Cemetery Road, then winding back on Moon Road and Lazy Lane.</p>
      <p>There are about 680 homes here on lots that average about 0.22 acre. Some are interior lots, and some are canal-front or lakefront with bulkheads and private docks. The community is not gated, and dues are low compared with other lake communities, a few hundred dollars a year in listings.</p>
      <p>The median home was built in 2004, some date back to the 1990s, and vacant lots are still for sale. That means we see both first fences on new builds and replacements on lots that have had the same fence for 20 years.</p>
