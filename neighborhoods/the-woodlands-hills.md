      <h2>Built into the woods between Conroe and Willis</h2>
      <p>Despite the name, this isn't part of The Woodlands. Howard Hughes broke ground on The Woodlands Hills in 2017, about 13 miles north of its better-known namesake, and held the grand opening in July 2018. The community covers about 2,000 acres on the west side of I-45, fronting FM 830 and stretching north toward FM 1097 and south toward League Line Road.</p>
      <p>The developer describes it as being in both Conroe and Willis. In practice, most mailing addresses read Willis 77318, and listings show Willis ISD schools. More than 4,500 homes are planned, and about 1,000 were in place by mid-2023, so plenty of sections are still filling in.</p>
      <p>The plan is built around trees and parks. It calls for 20 neighborhood parks, aiming for one within walking distance of every home, along with 9.5 miles of hike-and-bike trails and about 112 acres of open space. Lot sizes vary widely, from compact production lots to larger wooded homesites, so there is no one-size answer to what fence fits.</p>
