      <h2>A neighborhood built around the course</h2>
      <p>Panorama Village covers about a square mile west of I-45, reached from League Line Rd and Thunderbird Dr. It grew up around what is now Panorama Golf Club, a 27-hole course played as three nines, with a clubhouse and the Greenbrier Grill. About 2,500 people live there, according to the 2020 census.</p>
      <p>That history shows in the lots. Many homes back to a fairway, streets are lined with mature trees, and older homes sit next to newer infill like the Panorama-Quail Creek section. The city also has three parks, a community pool, and a pavilion, and Lake Conroe is close by.</p>
      <p>For a fence, the main questions are what the city allows, how to keep a course view, and how to build under big trees.</p>
