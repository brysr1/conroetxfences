      <h2>Twenty years in, the fence line tells the story</h2>
      <p>Most of Silverstone&rsquo;s homes went up around the mid-2000s. Wood fences built then have had close to two decades of Gulf Coast humidity, summer heat, and soggy ground after storms, and you can see it in leaning runs and gates that drag. At least one recent listing in the neighborhood mentions a replaced fence, and more owners are weighing the same decision.</p>
      <p>The lots make it a bigger decision than in most subdivisions. Silverstone has about 150 homes on roughly one-acre lots behind a gated entrance off Sgt. Ed Holcomb Blvd S, and some lots back to greenbelt. Rebuilding a perimeter on an acre is a project worth planning carefully.</p>
      <p>A small note: some listings show the postal city as &ldquo;Houston 77304.&rdquo; It&rsquo;s the same Silverstone in Conroe. Don&rsquo;t confuse it with a Silverstone Lane in The Woodlands.</p>
