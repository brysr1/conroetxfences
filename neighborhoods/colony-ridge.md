      <p>Colony Ridge covers about 33,000 to 34,000 acres, mostly in unincorporated Liberty County near Plum Grove, and uses Cleveland mailing addresses. Work began around 2011 and 2012. The land is split into six subdivisions (Santa Fe, Camino Real, Grand San Jacinto, Rancho San Vicente, Montebello, and Bella Vista) with roughly 42,000 lots.</p>
      <p>Lots are sold as land, and owners put up their own homes, manufactured homes, or builder homes, depending on the section. That means a lot of fence jobs start on raw ground: a perimeter line, a gate at the road, and later a yard fence closer to the house.</p>
      <p>The development has drawn state and federal legal and political attention.</p>
