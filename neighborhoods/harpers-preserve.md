    <h2>Before you fence in Harper&rsquo;s Preserve</h2>
    <h3>Start with the association</h3>
    <p>The community has two associations. The Harper&rsquo;s Preserve Community Association enforces the deed restrictions, and a separate property owners&rsquo; association runs events. Fence changes go through the deed-restriction side. We haven&rsquo;t found a published set of fence guidelines, so we never assume a height, style, or stain. We get the current requirements for that property and assemble the drawing and material details for the submittal.</p>
    <h3>Trees, roots, and shade</h3>
    <p>Much of the community is heavily wooded. We walk the line looking for large roots, low limbs, and spots that stay wet, and we shift posts a little rather than cut into a root zone. On wood fences in shady yards, sealing the boards early pays off.</p>
    <h3>Water and drainage</h3>
    <p>With a lake, a creek-fed preserve, and MUD-served drainage, some lots carry easements along the back or sides. We check the plat so the fence stays out of any area that has to remain open.</p>
    <h3>City limits and 811</h3>
    <p>Many subdivisions in this part of south Montgomery County are outside Conroe city limits. We check which rules govern your address before quoting, and no shovel goes in the ground until Texas 811 has marked the lines.</p>
    <p class="lc-note">Association rules and local requirements change from time to time. We confirm the current rules for your lot before quoting.</p>
