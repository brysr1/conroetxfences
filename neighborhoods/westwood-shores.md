      <h2>A small town behind a gate</h2>
      <p>Westwood Shores sits on Lake Livingston in Trinity County, with a Trinity 75862 address. It was developed in the 1970s and now covers about 1,300 acres with more than 800 homes and roughly 26 miles of roads. Entry is gated and staffed around the clock.</p>
      <p>The property owners association owns and runs the 18-hole golf course and the marina. There are also inland lakes, including Westwood Lake, plus a pool, courts, a fitness center, and a clubhouse.</p>
