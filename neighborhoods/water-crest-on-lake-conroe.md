      <p>D.R. Horton started Water Crest in 2012. It covers about 330 acres with about 860 lots in 15 sections, south of League Line Road and west of Longmire Road. Homes have a Conroe 77304 address, but the community is zoned to Willis ISD. A typical lot is about 8,000 square feet, with some larger ones.</p>
      <p>Amenities include a private boat dock and ramp, two pools, a dog park, and several fishing ponds. The Royal Cay section is gated with controlled access; the rest of the community is not described as gated.</p>
