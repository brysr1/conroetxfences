      <h2>Before you fence in Mavera</h2>
      <p class="lc-lede">A few minutes of homework keeps a small-lot fence from turning into a redo.</p>
    </div>
    <ul class="lc-checks">
      <li><strong>HOA review.</strong> Mavera has an HOA, and listings name Inframark as the manager. We haven&rsquo;t seen published fence rules, so we pull the current guidelines for your section and build the submittal around them.</li>
      <li><strong>Pond and trail lots.</strong> Some homes back to ponds. Rear lines facing water or open space can carry their own requirements, so we check before we pick a style.</li>
      <li><strong>Drainage between houses.</strong> Narrow side yards carry roof and yard runoff. We keep the bottom of the fence clear of the swale so water keeps moving and boards stay drier.</li>
      <li><strong>City or county.</strong> We confirm whether your address falls inside Conroe city limits or in the county, since that decides whether a city permit is part of the job.</li>
      <li><strong>Texas 811.</strong> New subdivisions have fresh utility runs close to property lines. We request locates every time before a post hole is dug.</li>
      <li><strong>Shared lines.</strong> On a lot this size, nearly every run touches a neighbor. We can quote a shared section so both sides know the cost up front.</li>
    </ul>
    <p class="lc-note">HOA guidelines and local requirements can change. We confirm the current rules for your lot before we quote and don&rsquo;t build until approvals are in hand.</p>
