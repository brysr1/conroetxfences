Streets wind over wooded hills, ground elevation changes by more than 150 feet from the low spots to the high ones, and much of the edge backs to forest and state land.</p>

      <p>For a fence builder, that adds up to three kinds of jobs: lots on the water, where the view matters; lots on a hill, where the fence has to follow the ground; and smaller in-town lots, where a privacy fence or a dog run is the main goal.</p>
