The Signorelli Company broke ground on its first 370-plus lots in 2023 and opened home sales in April 2024, on 256 acres where Airport Road meets FM 1484, close to the Conroe-North Houston Regional Airport. About 850 homes are planned, built at launch by CastleRock Communities, Rausch Coleman Homes, and Brightland Homes.</p>
      <p>It's priced at the entry level, and the lots are compact: 40, 45, and 50 feet wide. Listings show homes sold with the back yard already fenced, so the work we do here is usually about getting more out of a small yard. That can mean a wider gate for a mower or trailer, a fence line that finally keeps the dog in, or a stain coat that protects new boards.</p>
      <p>Cielo also has more open space than its size suggests. There are more than 45 acres of green space and lakes, including a 5.5-acre amenity lake, plus a dog park and walking paths, and Bartlett Elementary sits inside the community.</p>

      <h2>Before you fence in Cielo</h2>

    <ul class="lc-checks lc-checks--one">
      <li><strong>HOA first.</strong> Listings show Inframark manages the Cielo HOA. We haven't seen published fence rules, so we pull the current guidelines and submit your plan before we start.</li>
      <li><strong>Check the lot edges.</strong> Lake-adjacent and green-space lots can be treated differently from lots that back to another yard. We look at the plat and what your rear line faces.</li>
      <li><strong>Mind the side yard.</strong> On a 40-foot lot, the space between houses is tight. Gate placement and swing direction need planning so they clear AC units and downspouts.</li>
      <li><strong>City limits.</strong> A 77303 address doesn't settle it. We check whether the lot is within the City of Conroe, which would add the city's permit step.</li>
      <li><strong>Texas 811.</strong> We request utility locates before every job, which matters in a new community with freshly buried lines.</li>
    </ul>
