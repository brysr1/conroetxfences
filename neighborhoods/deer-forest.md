      <h2>Fencing through the woods</h2>
      <p>A straight fence line through pines and underbrush takes some clearing. We cut what is needed to set posts and stretch wire, keep the bigger trees where we can, and bend the line around them when that makes more sense than cutting.</p>
      <p>We also look for low spots and drainage paths on the walk. Wire fences let water through easily, but a privacy fence across a wet swale can wash out at the bottom.</p>
