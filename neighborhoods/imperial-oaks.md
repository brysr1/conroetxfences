      <p>We serve it because it is an easy run down I-45 from our office, and because the neighborhood has a lot of fence work waiting. The median home here was built in 1994, so many back yards still have their original wood fences, or a second fence that is now also aging.</p>
      <p>The community covers about 1,400 acres, with more than 150 acres set aside for recreation: five parks, three nature preserves, a 43-acre wooded preserve with greenbelt trails, a 9-acre sports complex, pools, and tennis courts. That means a lot of lots back up to trees.</p>
