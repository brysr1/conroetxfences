      <h2>Where the address and the map disagree</h2>
      <p>Mail for Waterwood goes to Huntsville 77320, so people often expect it to be on the edge of town. It isn&rsquo;t. The community is in northern San Jacinto County on the northwest shore of Lake Livingston, around FM 980 and Waterwood Parkway, and Huntsville is about 27 miles away.</p>
      <p>Horizon Properties started Waterwood in 1972. It covers about 8,000 acres with roughly 2,100 single-family lots, many of them wooded and some right on the water. The Waterwood Improvement Association looks after the community, and covenants differ from section to section: Augusta Estates, Bay Hill, Country Club Estates, Lakeview Estates, Whispering Pines, and others.</p>
      <p>Listed lots run from under a quarter acre to more than an acre, with many around half an acre. That&rsquo;s enough room for a real yard fence and, on waterfront lots, a decision about how much of the lake you want to keep in view.</p>
