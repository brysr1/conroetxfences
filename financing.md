      <h2>How it works</h2>
      <p>76 Fence of Conroe works with <strong>Hearth</strong>, a financing company, for customers who prefer to pay over time. Hearth has plans for applicants with a credit score of 550 or higher. Applicants who qualify with a score of 680 or above may be offered 0% financing.</p>

      <h2>When financing makes sense</h2>
      <p>Some fence jobs cost more than people plan for. That might be a full replacement of an aging builder fence, an HOA-approved upgrade to a better material, or a long perimeter run on acreage. Financing lets you build the fence you actually want now, without cutting the scope down to fit what you have on hand today.</p>
