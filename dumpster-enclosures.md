faqs:
  - q: "Does my Conroe business need a dumpster enclosure?"
    a: "Often, but not always. It depends on whether the site is inside Conroe city limits, what the approved site plan says, and any property-owner or landlord rules. We confirm the requirements for your address before we design anything."
  - q: "How wide should the gates be?"
    a: "Wide enough for your hauler's truck to reach the container without hitting a post. That depends on the container size and the truck, so we check with you or the hauler before we set the gate posts."
  - q: "Can the enclosure match our building?"
    a: "In most cases, yes. We can match an existing wood or vinyl fence, or pick a color and panel style that suits the building."
  - q: "Can you repair a damaged enclosure?"
    a: "Yes. Bent gate frames, broken drop rods, and posts knocked loose by containers are common repairs. Where an enclosure keeps getting hit, we can upgrade the posts or add steel bollards to protect them."
