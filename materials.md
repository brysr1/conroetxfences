      <p class="lc-kicker">How to choose</p>
      <h2>Choosing for Conroe conditions</h2>
      <p>Conroe weather and ground narrow the choices quickly. Start with these questions.</p>
      <ul class="lc-checks lc-checks--one">
        <li><strong>How wet does the yard get?</strong> Long humid summers and heavy spring rain are hard on wood that sits close to the ground. Low lots near creeks or the river call for a rot board, good drainage under the fence, or a material that does not soak up water.</li>
        <li><strong>What is the soil like?</strong> Montgomery County ground ranges from loose sand to sticky clay, sometimes on one block. Post depth and footing size matter as much as the material you pick.</li>
        <li><strong>Is it a lake or canal lot?</strong> Open aluminum or steel keeps the view and holds up near the water. On Lake Conroe, we also check where SJRA land starts before planning the layout.</li>
        <li><strong>Are there big trees?</strong> On wooded lots, falling limbs are part of life. Wood and chain link are easy to repair one section at a time.</li>
        <li><strong>What does your HOA allow?</strong> Many communities limit the material, height, or color. We confirm the current rules for your lot before you choose.</li>
        <li><strong>Are there animals to keep in?</strong> Dogs, horses, and other livestock each call for different wire, spacing, and post layouts.</li>
      </ul>
