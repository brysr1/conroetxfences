    <p><strong>Last updated:</strong> October 12, 2026</p>

    <p>This policy covers information that 76 Fence of Conroe (&ldquo;we,&rdquo; &ldquo;our,&rdquo; &ldquo;us&rdquo;) receives through the website conroetxfences.com. It also describes how we use and share that information and the choices you have.</p>

    <h2>What you share with us</h2>
    <p>If you use the contact form, schedule an estimate, or reach us by phone or text, you may share your name, a phone number and email, the property&rsquo;s address or neighborhood, and a description of your project. We use that information to respond to you, set up and prepare your estimate, complete any work you hire us to do, and check in with you afterward.</p>

    <h2>What is collected automatically</h2>
    <p>When you visit, this site and the services it uses receive some basic technical data, as most websites do. That can include your IP address, your browser and device type, the pages you open, and the page that sent you here.</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Google Analytics, if enabled.</strong> When analytics is turned on for this site, we use Google Analytics to see how people use the site so we can make it better. Google Analytics relies on cookies and similar technologies. Google describes its use of that data here: <a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites">policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites</a>. You can opt out with Google&rsquo;s <a href="https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout">Analytics opt-out browser add-on</a>.</li>
      <li><strong>Hosting.</strong> GitHub Pages hosts the site and may record visitors&rsquo; IP addresses for security and to keep the service running.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Outside services we use</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Contact form.</strong> Formspree delivers messages from our contact form to us.</li>
      <li><strong>Online booking.</strong> &ldquo;Book a Free Estimate&rdquo; links open our client hub on Jobber. Jobber handles what you enter there on our behalf.</li>
      <li><strong>Maps.</strong> Some pages have embedded Google Maps, which can set cookies and collect data under Google&rsquo;s privacy policy.</li>
      <li><strong>Photos.</strong> Some images load from Unsplash, which may receive basic technical data from your browser when they load.</li>
      <li><strong>Financing.</strong> If you apply for financing, Hearth receives your application, and Hearth&rsquo;s own terms and privacy policy apply.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Those companies follow their own privacy policies for anything they collect.</p>

    <h2>Sharing</h2>
    <p>We do not sell personal information. We share it only in these cases: with the providers above when the site or the business depends on it, with other 76 Fence companies if your project calls for it, at your request, or where the law requires it.</p>

    <h2>Cookies and your choices</h2>
    <p>Your browser settings let you block or clear cookies. Without cookies the site still loads, but analytics and embedded maps might not work. To correct or delete the contact details you gave us, call us or write to the address below.</p>

    <h2>Children</h2>
    <p>This site is meant for adults planning a fence project. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.</p>

    <h2>Updates</h2>
    <p>We may change this policy from time to time. The &ldquo;last updated&rdquo; date at the top shows when the most recent change was made.</p>
