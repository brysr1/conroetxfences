faqs:
  - q: "Can you replace the fence the builder put in?"
    a: "Yes. Builder fences in newer Conroe communities are often the first thing to lean or gray. We can match the existing height and style so the line still looks like the rest of the street, or upgrade posts and boards if your HOA allows it. We confirm the current rules for your lot before quoting."
  - q: "Do I need a permit for a backyard fence?"
    a: "It depends on the address. Lots inside Conroe city limits fall under the city's Building Inspections and Permits department. Many subdivisions around Conroe are in unincorporated Montgomery County, and Panorama Village is its own city. We check which rules apply to your property before we build."
  - q: "How long does a typical backyard fence take?"
    a: "Most backyard fences on a standard subdivision lot are built in a few days once approvals and 811 locates are done. Long runs, steep grade, heavy tree cover, or several gates add time, and we give you a realistic schedule with the quote."
  - q: "Can you fence a yard that backs up to a pond, greenbelt, or the lake?"
    a: "Yes, though these lots often have extra limits. Communities usually want open fencing along water and greenbelts, and on Lake Conroe the land up to the 201-ft contour belongs to the San Jacinto River Authority, which requires a permit for permanent structures. We sort out where the fence can go before we set a post."
