  - q: "How tall are ornamental fences?"
    a: "Four to five feet is typical for yards and pools, with taller panels at entries and along estate frontage. Your community's guidelines and any city rules for the lot also come into it, and we check both."
  - q: "Will an ornamental fence hold my dog?"
    a: "For most medium and large dogs, yes. Small dogs can slip between standard pickets, so we offer panels with tighter spacing or extra pickets at the bottom."

    <h2>A formal edge you can see through</h2>
    <p>Ornamental fencing is the style; <a href="/materials/ornamental-steel-aluminum/">aluminum and steel</a> are the materials. Vertical pickets run between two or three rails, with flat tops, spear points, or finials, and gates that can be arched or flat.</p>
    <p>It shows up wherever the view matters: in front of a two-story home, along a golf hole, around a pool, or at the back of a lake lot. It looks the same from both sides and doesn&rsquo;t compete with the landscaping.</p>
  </div>
</section>

      <p class="lc-kicker">Local conditions</p>
      <h2>Humidity, sun, and storms</h2>
      <p><strong>Near water:</strong> aluminum doesn&rsquo;t rust, so it is our usual pick for lake and pool lots.</p>
      <p><strong>Sun:</strong> a good powder coat keeps its finish through Texas summers; a rinse a couple of times a year keeps it clean.</p>
      <p><strong>Storms:</strong> wind passes through, and a panel bent by a limb can be replaced by itself.</p>
      <p><strong>Ground:</strong> posts set in concrete for the lot, after a Texas 811 locate.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="lc-callout"><p><strong>Lake Conroe lots:</strong> land up to the 201-foot contour around the lake belongs to the San Jacinto River Authority, and permanent structures there need an SJRA permit. We confirm where your property ends before setting posts near the shoreline.</p></div>
