faqs:
  - q: "How much can people see through a shadowbox fence?"
    a: "Straight on, very little. From an angle you get glimpses through the offset boards. It is close to full privacy while still letting air move."
  - q: "Do gaps really help in storms?"
    a: "Yes. Air passing through the gaps takes load off the posts compared with a solid wall of boards of the same height."
  - q: "Can I add a lattice top to an existing fence?"
    a: "Sometimes, if the posts are long enough and still sound. If not, we can replace the posts and reuse a run of good panels below the new top."

      <p class="lc-kicker">The idea</p>
      <h2>Mostly private, never stuffy</h2>
      <p>A semi-privacy fence hides most of what&rsquo;s behind it but lets some light and air through. It sits between a solid privacy fence and an open picket or rail fence.</p>
      <p>On a Conroe summer afternoon, a solid six-foot wall can trap heat and humidity in a small yard. A fence with a little give lets the breeze through, keeps flower beds along the line from sitting in deep shade, and looks lighter against a greenbelt or a pond.</p>
      <p>Most run five to six feet tall.</p>
