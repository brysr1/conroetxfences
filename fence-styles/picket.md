  - q: "How tall is a typical picket fence?"
    a: "Usually three to four feet. That's tall enough to mark the yard and slow down a small dog, and low enough to keep the house in view."
  - q: "Can I put a picket fence in my front yard?"
    a: "Front-yard fences are the ones communities are most likely to regulate, and inside Conroe city limits the city's rules also apply. We check your HOA and the rules for your address before you commit."

      <h2>A border, not a barrier</h2>
      <p>Picket fences are about three to four feet tall, with evenly spaced vertical boards on two rails. They don&rsquo;t hide anything. What they do is draw a line around a front yard, a vegetable garden, or a walkway, and give toddlers and small dogs a clear edge.</p>
      <p>The details make the fence: the shape of the picket tops, how close they sit, the posts and caps, and a gate that matches. Around Conroe we see them most on older homes with deeper front yards, cottage-style houses, and garden plots behind the house.</p>
