faqs:
  - q: "What are the rules for a pool fence?"
    a: "Requirements depend on the jurisdiction your property falls under, and your HOA and insurer may add their own. Common items include a minimum height, limits on gaps between pickets, and self-closing, self-latching gates. We look up the rules for your address before we build."
  - q: "What does self-closing, self-latching mean?"
    a: "The gate swings shut on its own after someone walks through and latches without anyone touching it. The latch usually sits high, out of a small child's reach, and the gate typically opens outward, away from the pool."
  - q: "Can my existing privacy fence count as a pool barrier?"
    a: "Sometimes, if its height, gaps, and gates meet the requirements that apply to you. Gates on older fences often don't, and we can usually retrofit self-closing hinges and a self-latching latch."
  - q: "Why aluminum around pools?"
    a: "Splash, pool chemicals, and humid air will rust exposed steel over time. Aluminum doesn't rust, and the open pickets let you see the pool from the house."

      <p>Most pool fences around Conroe are black aluminum: it doesn&rsquo;t rust from splash, and you can watch the pool from the kitchen window. Where neighbors are close, a privacy section on one side can work alongside it.</p>
      <p>The gate is the part that matters most. A pool fence is only as safe as the gate that closes behind the last person through it.</p>

      <h2>Upkeep around the pool</h2>
      <ul class="lc-checks lc-checks--one">
        <li><strong>Gate check each month.</strong> Open the gate, let go, and make sure it closes and latches on its own. Humidity and heat can stiffen hinges.</li>
        <li><strong>Rinse off splash.</strong> Chlorine and salt residue wash off with a hose.</li>
        <li><strong>After storms.</strong> Look for limbs on panels and anything that props a gate open.</li>
        <li><strong>Keep it clear.</strong> Chairs, planters, and pool equipment next to the fence can become a step stool.</li>
        <li><strong>Before digging.</strong> Pool yards have buried lines; we request Texas 811 locates first.</li>
