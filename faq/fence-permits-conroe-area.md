<div class="lc-callout">
  <p><strong>Short answer:</strong> It depends on which government your lot actually sits in, and the mailing address won't tell you. Inside Conroe, Willis, Panorama Village, Huntsville, or Cleveland, the city's fence rules apply. Out in unincorporated Montgomery County, a fence outside the floodplain generally doesn't need a county permit, though deed restrictions, an HOA, and SJRA or TRA rules on lake lots still can.</p>
</div>

<h2>Step one: find your jurisdiction</h2>
<p>Conroe ZIP codes reach far past the city limits. A few examples from the neighborhoods we work in:</p>
<ul>
  <li><a href="/neighborhoods/teaswood/">Teaswood</a> has Conroe addresses and Willis ISD schools, and local tax records place it inside Conroe city limits.</li>
  <li><a href="/neighborhoods/river-plantation/">River Plantation</a>, just south of town, is unincorporated Montgomery County, so the city permit office doesn't normally cover it.</li>
  <li><a href="/neighborhoods/lake-chateau-woods/">Lake Chateau Woods</a> was once the City of Chateau Woods and is now unincorporated county.</li>
  <li><a href="/neighborhoods/panorama-village/">Panorama Village</a> has been its own city since 1972, even though its mail says Conroe.</li>
  <li>Several east-shore lake communities with Willis addresses, such as Point Aquarius and Seven Coves, appear to sit outside Willis city limits.</li>
</ul>
<p>Your appraisal district record or a call to the city settles it, and we confirm it before the quote is final.</p>

<h2>Who files, and what you pay</h2>
<p>Where a city lets the contractor apply, we're glad to. Some offices want the owner's signature or the owner's own submission. Either way, we draw the fence line on your survey or plot plan and hand you a packet that's ready to file. If we pay the city's fee, it shows on your quote as a pass-through, not a markup.</p>
<p>Panorama Village adds a step for the builder: its <a href="https://panoramavillagetx.gov/resident_services/planning_and_zoning.php" rel="noopener">contractor registration</a> requires a general liability certificate naming the city before anyone can work there. Typical installed prices, before any permit fee, are on our <a href="/pricing-guide/">pricing guide</a>.</p>

<h2>The layers on top of a permit</h2>
<p>A city permit and an HOA approval are separate reviews. In Grand Central Park, Artavia, Mavera, or Harper's Preserve, the architectural committee looks at the fence whatever the city requires. On the Lake Conroe shore, SJRA's <a href="https://www.sjra.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Lake-Conroe-Rules-and-Regulations-2015.pdf" rel="noopener">rules</a> govern land below the 201-foot contour. On Lake Livingston, shoreline work goes through the <a href="https://trinityra.org/doing_business_with_us/property_owner_permits_and_licenses_at_lake_livingston.php" rel="noopener">Trinity River Authority</a>, and the lot itself may be in Polk, San Jacinto, Walker, or Trinity County. See <a href="/faq/flood-zone-easement-fencing/">flood zones and easements</a> for those limits.</p>
<p>Pools add one more set. Cleveland has adopted the 2015 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code, and Panorama Village requires pool fences under its adopted codes. Our <a href="/fence-styles/pool-enclosure/">pool enclosure</a> page covers the build side. A powered drive gate can also bring an electrical permit; inside Conroe, the city's electrical application requires a state-licensed electrician registered with the city.</p>
